# Venn view in Student mode (2026-11-06)

Goal: in Student mode, show a Venn diagram of the patient's symptoms and test results across the
three best-matching candidates, so the learner sees which evidence each disease explains, what they
share, and what none of them explains.

Interpretation: three circles = the top three candidates on the differential (by fit). Every finding
sits in the region of the diseases that explain it (same rule as the fit score: the disease has the
term or a child of it, or a parent at reduced weight). Symptoms (history, examination, course,
treatment) are filled chips, test results (laboratory, imaging, specialist) outlined chips.
