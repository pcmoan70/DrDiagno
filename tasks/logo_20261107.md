# Logo and icon for Dr. Diagno (2026-11-07)

Request: "Add a logo/icon to the app: inspired by dr house and sherlock holmes".

## Design
Original mark, no likeness of either character or show. First draft was an object mark (deerstalker
on a lens with a cane handle); the user asked for a person silhouette, then stressed "detective", then
"too many details, make it simple". Final mark: two flat shapes (profile bust, deerstalker) plus a
magnifying glass with a red cross in the lens. Dropped on the way: cane, hat band and knot,
stethoscope, coat collar, ECG trace. Fourth round: "hat should be more Sherlock Holmes like", so the
hat became a low crown with a stiff visor front and back and the ear flaps tied in a bow on top.
Fifth round: "The hat should be a deerstalker": thicker cap-style visors, the ear flap drawn tied up
against the crown (pale outline), bow loops tucked behind the crown so they read as a knot, not ears. Palette from the app theme: slate, amber accent, red trace, pale lens so
the shape reads on light and dark tab bars. Hand-written SVG, 64x64 viewBox, no text.
