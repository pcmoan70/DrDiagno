Dr. Diagno

A patient walks in with symptoms that don't add up. The clock is running. Build a differential, order the right tests, and name the disease before it's too late.

Every case is generated from real medical knowledge: 7,000 diseases and 20,000 clinical findings from the Human Phenotype Ontology, Orphanet and Mondo. Entertainment and education only, not medical advice.

Watch-along

No patient, no clock, no score. Type findings as you hear them, say while watching a medical drama, and see which of 7,000 diseases fit. Also a study tool.

            Venn: what your best candidates explain

            Each circle is one of your three best-matching candidates. Every finding sits in the region of the diseases that explain it; the numbers count them, hover one to see which.
